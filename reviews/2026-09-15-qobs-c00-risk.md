## Summary

Scored the targeted FR-019 successor before retrospective task decomposition.
The change introduces no new evaluator, concurrency, persistence, security, or
performance guarantee; its risk is contract skew at an external exact-pinned
owner boundary.

| Req | Tech Risk | Volatility | Drivers | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Top hazards

1. A compatibility wrapper could recreate observation or temporal semantics
   instead of delegating to FR-018; byte/usage parity and one-over refusal make
   that substitution observable.
2. Repair or query artifacts could leak into TL and become an accidental second
   semantic owner; the closed compatibility selector accepts no foreign value.
3. Cargo resolution, public provenance, fixtures, and documentation could name
   different revisions; TC-085 and the shared pin/source gates bind those facts.
