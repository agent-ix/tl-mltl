# tl-mltl v0.1 source-release argument

## Claim

The open claim concerns only the exact source, syntax dependency, and corpus
revisions presented to the human owner. It excludes downstream monitor
qualification and crates.io publication.

## Reasoning

Complete semantic, resource, prefix, mapping, and differential obligations
reduce uncertainty for the bounded intended use. Contextual calls additionally
bind the exact shared signal catalog and caller context rather than inferring
either. Human review owns sufficiency.

## Sufficiency Decision

The claim remains open until the sealed change-assurance record names the exact
candidate, all blocking gaps close, and the human owner records an explicit
decision. Automated checks cannot change this status.

## Challenges

The `tl-syntax` supply-chain exception is discharged. The compiled pin is now
`953ee825e5060335b4c79682f5f41a78c5a1bfae`, which is reachable from tl-syntax
`main`, so the exception that permitted a pull-request revision no longer
applies and its 2026-09-07 expiry is moot. The corpus basis deliberately did not
move: `corpus/tl-syntax-v1` remains the byte-identical copy taken at
`740182f13b84858008d6f176f75136737d405c1b` and verified by its own `SHA256SUMS`.
Both revisions are declared in `assurance/pins.json` and cross-checked on every
run, and collapsing them into one string is refused.

External R2U2 availability and project-specific monitor qualification remain
outside this source-release claim and cannot be closed by local emulation. The
retained R2U2 4.2 exchange is evidence of agreement at one external revision. No
gate re-executes R2U2 or C2PO, deliberately, so a change in external behaviour
after `4.2-release` would not be observed here.

Preserving caller-supplied requirement context establishes byte identity and
comparison consistency only. It does not establish that the requirement,
revision, clause, anchor, or span is truthful, does not validate the future
contract-IR/FRETish adapter, and does not qualify the mapped monitor.

**The Make execution-control guard is gone and this argument does not pretend
otherwise.** The parse-time guard block in the `Makefile` and
`scripts/check_failure_propagation.py` were removed with the collector they were
protecting. The class they guarded is real and was measured in this repository:
with a syntax error introduced into `src/lib.rs`, `make -k ci` exits 2 and 10
of the 14 `ci` prerequisites do not complete while 4 still do; adding a single
`.IGNORE:` line makes all 10 report success and `make ci` exits 0. What replaces the guard is
structural — Quoin binds each retained input by digest and every attested result
is derived from the producer's own bytes, so a producer that did not run yields
an absent or empty input the chain refuses — and it covers only the six proofs
re-run inside `make assurance-inputs`. `fmt-check`, `lint`, `check-corpus`,
`deny`, `audit-unsafe`, `rustdoc`, and the `quire validate` half of `spec` feed
no input to the chain and are simply neutered; `.IGNORE:` also suppresses the
chain's own refusal. This is recorded as an open challenge above, as an open
unknown in `assurance/change-assurance.json`, and as `agent-ix/tl-mltl#14`. It is
not claimed to be closed.

The host-scoped executable census in `tools.lock`, and the claims this argument
previously made about it, are withdrawn along with the file. A second party can
now reproduce every gate: nothing in the gate set depends on one operator's
absolute paths or home directory. That is a strengthening of reproducibility and
a weakening of executable-identity binding at the same time, and both halves are
stated.

## Retained Archive — released and deleted

There is no retained archive. The repository owner decided on 2026-09-02 to
release the evidence-preservation constraint for the pre-stable phase, recorded
in `agent-ix/engineering-assurance#7` under "Preservation constraint released for
the pre-stable phase". Under that decision the six records — 283 files, 842,668
bytes, including `tl-mltl-v01-da2c7704a534-20260831T052229Z` — their reader,
their fixtures and the two frozen schemas were deleted under
`agent-ix/tl-mltl#16`.

Nothing was rewritten, backdated or re-sealed to look as though it still
verifies. None of those records supported this claim while they existed: read
through `map_pgm01_bytes` at the pinned release, all six classified
`incompatible`. The claims this argument previously made from them are deleted
with them and are not restated in a weaker form. The constraint re-applies
unchanged at the move toward stable releases, and evidence retained under it
from that point is immutable.

The claim remains open. It is not closed by this migration, and no local digest,
sealed record, or verification receipt in this repository confers a release
decision. The receipt this change produces reads `incomplete` with a missing
human decision, which is the correct answer while no ix-flow decision event
exists.
