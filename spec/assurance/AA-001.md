## Challenges

policy only while PR #6 awaits human disposition. The exception expires on
2026-09-07 or when PR #6 is merged or revised, whichever comes first. tl-mltl
must then repin, rerun its evidence, and remain blocked from source release
until the human owner accepts the resulting exact dependency revision.

outside this source-release claim and cannot be closed by local emulation.

than a portable build environment. A second party can verify the sealed record,
its source-derived parameters, and its recorded tool digests without possessing
those paths; only a new qualification collection requires the named host profile.

consumer's compiler, runtime, or deployment environment.

paths and digests under an allowlisted environment. Its Cargo artifacts are
built from a freshly cleared, ignored `.qualification-target` directory inside
the repository. A retained record is active only while its exact source revision
remains reachable from a branch or tag, and local verification fails when every
archive is retracted or otherwise inactive.
