# tl-mltl v0.1 reference-evaluator assurance profile

## Decision Boundary

Evidence supports a human decision about one exact source candidate. It does
not qualify R2U2, a generated mapping, a consuming monitor, or any system.

## Impact Scenarios

The material scenarios are an incorrect reference verdict and an understated
horizon or buffer bound. Both can be checked deterministically before release.

## Evidence Policy

Evidence identifies formula/profile/trace bytes, dependency and tool revisions,
limits, outcomes, limitations, and requirement tags. Pending, unsupported,
failed, and skipped results remain non-conclusive. Only `@kreneskyp` may approve
the source release.

## Exceptions

No exception is implicit. A bounded exception must identify its owner, affected
criterion, rationale, expiry, and human acceptance record.
